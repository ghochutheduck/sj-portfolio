## Case Studies

### 1. Lombardi Hotels — Brand Identity + Social Media Campaign
- **When/where:** Summer 2024, Gabicce Mare, Italy
- **Role:** Brand Strategy · Identity · Campaign · Character Design
- **Brief:** Rebuild awareness with new guests while creating one cohesive brand across the three properties.
- **Results:**
  - 673 Italian reservations
  - 3,003 Italian room nights
  - 44.7% of searches were couples travelling together
  - 38.9% were families booking as a group
- **Closing line:** "The project gave Lombardi a cohesive identity across its properties and a campaign rooted in the people and place of Gabicce Mare."

### 2. BoopShaillyBoop — An art mail club
- **Description:** One envelope a month, made by hand, sent from India.
- **When:** 2026
- **Role:** Brand, product, build, operations
- **Note:** "I built this for people my own age, and I was wrong about that. The largest group signing up has been people between 40 and 60, who grew up writing letters and already understood the weight of a stamped envelope."
- **Result:** "A hundred members in a year, and a club where people look forward to each other's art as much as mine."

### 3. SANAT — 400-year-old wood carving tradition, repackaged
- **When/context:** Graduation Project, World University of Design, Sonepat, 2025; sponsored by the Indian Institute of Packaging
- **Role:** Branding Strategy · Visual Identity · Packaging Design
- **Result:** "It was built. Four boxes, a handle, and a building you can carry home."
- **Stats:**
  - B.Des — Graduation thesis, World University of Design, 2025
  - IIP — Sponsored by the Indian Institute of Packaging
  - ODOP — Built under the UP Government's One District One Product initiative
  - 400+ yrs of heritage the brand is built to carry forward

### 4. XFC Cricket — Practice Kit 2026/27
